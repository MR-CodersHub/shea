The Origin of shea

A philosophy of handmade skincare and natural wellness born out of reverence for the earth. Every product is a testament to purity, craft, and intentional beauty.

Our Mission

To transform daily skincare routines into moments of pure, natural luxury while protecting our earth's resources. We believe in transparency over trends, and clean ingredients over complex formulations.

Our Vision

A world where the beauty industry works in perfect harmony with nature, leaving no trace behind but glowing, healthy skin and revitalized ecosystems. Beauty with a conscience.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make

Authenticity

Real ingredients, real results. No marketing fluff or false promises.

Sustainability

Ethical sourcing and zero-waste production at the heart of everything we do.

Quality

Handcrafted in small batches with obsessive attention to detail.

Meet Elena

"I started shea in my kitchen, driven by a frustration with the complex, chemical-laden ingredients in mainstream beauty. I wanted to create a return to the botanical roots of skincare—to honor both nature and the human body."

With over 15 years of experience in botanical formulation and a background in organic chemistry, Elena has dedicated her life to creating skincare products that are as effective as they are pure.

Elena Rostova
Founder & Master Formulator
